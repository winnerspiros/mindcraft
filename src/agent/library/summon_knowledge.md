# Summoning knowledge — mobs, what they do, drops, how to fight and survive them

You summon through !summon (trust-gated: strangers get charm, never powers).
There is deliberately NO count cap in code — the number is YOUR call every time.
One is cute; a pile lags the whole server, wrecks builds, and kills players who
never agreed to fight. Judge each ask: who is near, what breaks, can it be undone.
Observe first with !searchForEntity(type, range); clean up your
own mess with !despawn(type, radius). Spawning near other players without their consent
is griefing — ask or move far away first.

## Bosses (never casual — each one can wreck builds and kill everyone nearby)
- wither: 300 HP. Big spawn explosion, flies, shoots exploding skulls that give wither
  effect (black hearts, drains health). Drops nether_star (beacon). Fight: full diamond+
  armor, smite bow from 30+ blocks, milk to clear wither effect, golden apples. Never
  spawn near home, spawn, or other players.
- ender_dragon: 200 HP, dragon breath + knockback, heals from end crystals. Drops
  dragon_egg + huge XP. Only meaningful in the End; spawning one in the overworld is
  pure destruction. Fight: bow, beds as bombs in the End, feather-falling boots.
- warden: 500 HP, lives in the deep dark, sonic boom goes THROUGH armor and walls,
  inflicts darkness. Drops sculk_catalyst. Strategy: DO NOT FIGHT — sneak, place wool
  to silence movement, throw snowballs to distract, run. One boom can one-shot you.

## Hostiles (common summons — know the counter before spawning)
- creeper: silent, 3-second fuse, explodes. Charged by lightning = double damage.
  Drops gunpowder (plus music disc if a skeleton lands the kill). Counter: bow, shield,
  or a cat nearby (creepers flee cats). Never melee without armor.
- zombie: burns in daylight, slow. Drops rotten_flesh (rare iron/carrot/potato).
  Counter: 2-block pillar + hit down, or any sword.
- skeleton: shoots arrows, strafes. Drops bones + arrows. Counter: shield up, rush
  between shots, zigzag at range. Its arrows can kill creepers for discs.
- spider: climbs walls, neutral in daylight unless attacked. Drops string + spider_eye.
  Counter: daylight is safe; at night keep distance, bow or hit-and-back.
- enderman: neutral until you look at its face or hit it; teleports behind you.
  Drops ender_pearl. Protection: carved pumpkin on head, stand under a 2-block roof,
  water/lava hurts it. Never stare.
- witch: throws poison/slowness/weakness, drinks healing potions. Drops sticks, glass,
  bottles, redstone. Counter: rush it down fast before it heals; milk clears effects.
- blaze (nether): flying, shoots fireballs. Drops blaze_rod (brewing + eyes of ender).
  Protection: fire resistance potion, shield; snowballs deal 3 damage each.
- ghast (nether): giant, shoots explosive fireballs, cries. Drops ghast_tear +
  gunpowder. Counter: punch/deflect the fireball back for a kill, or bow.
- wither_skeleton (nether fortress): fast, inflicts wither on hit. Drops coal, bones,
  rarely a skull (3 skulls + soul sand = a wither — think before collecting).
  Counter: smite sword, milk, never fight groups in the open.
- phantom: spawns after 3+ nights without sleep, swoops from the sky. Drops
  phantom_membrane (repairs elytra). Prevention: just SLEEP. Counter: bed + bow,
  or hit it mid-swoop.
- slime / magma_cube: splits into smaller ones when killed. Drops slimeball (leads,
  sticky pistons) / magma_cream (fire res potions). Counter: kill smallest first,
  magma cubes immune to lava.
- piglin (nether): neutral ONLY if you wear gold armor; piglin_brute always hostile.
  Barters gold for ender pearls and more. Counter: one gold piece minimum, never open
  chests near them.
- hoglin (nether): charges, throws you. Drops porkchop + leather. Counter: bow from a
  pillar, or warped fungus on a stick to repel.
- pillager / vindicator / evoker (raids): crossbows and axes; evoker summons vexes and
  drops totem_of_undying (saves you from death — hold in offhand). Counter: shield wall,
  pick evokers first, keep villagers indoors.
- tnt (primed entity, not a mob): 4-second fuse, destroys blocks, chains to nearby TNT.
  Water absorbs the blast. Light from distance with flint and steel, never by hand.

## Passives and friends (safe summons — your cute-treat substitutes)
- cow: leather + beef, milk removes ALL effects (carry a bucket). pig: porkchop, rides
  with saddle + carrot on a stick. sheep: wool + mutton (dyeable). chicken: feathers +
  eggs. Feed two of a kind wheat/seeds/carrots to breed.
- wolf: tame with bones, fights FOR you, sits on command. cat: tame with fish, scares
  creepers away, brings morning gifts. parrot: sits on shoulder, dances to music.
- villager: trades emeralds for gear — protect with walls and light, zombies infect them.
- iron_golem: spawns to protect villagers, 100 HP, launches mobs sky-high. Drops iron +
  poppy. NEVER hit one. snow_golem: throws snowballs, melts in heat/rain — cute guard.
- horse/donkey/llama: tame, saddle, ride; donkey + chest = mobile storage.

## Observing and cleaning up
- Before summoning anything hostile: !searchForEntity to see what is already around
  (players? villagers? their builds?) — context decides whether spawning is okay.
- After a fight or a joke: !despawn the leftovers. Roaming withers killed a newcomer
  hours after the joke ended — the summoner owns the cleanup.
- Yourself: armor on, shield in offhand against skeletons, food above 6 drumsticks,
  milk + golden apples for bosses, feather falling near heights, fire res in the nether,
  pumpkin in the End, bed set before any boss attempt.
